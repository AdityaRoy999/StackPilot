STACKPILOT / SETUP

Your platform, on your computer.

Three steps: prepare your computer, start StackPilot, then connect your AI provider. Your existing credentials and projects are preserved.

1. Check your computer

Install missing tools using the official links, then start Docker. Some operating systems require administrator approval or a restart. First builds need several GB of free storage.

    2. Start your platform

    Local installation at localhost:3000. Use the documented server installer for public HTTPS hosting. Existing installations retain their service profile for updates.

    3. Create your account and connect AI

    Open the dashboard after the health checks pass. The first-launch guide saves your provider key and tests a real model response. AI setup can be skipped for deployment-only use.

    Open dashboard

    Keep the launcher terminal open while setup is running. Reopen it whenever you need to check or update your installation.
