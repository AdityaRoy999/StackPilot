## Frame review and corrections

Inspected the opening, brand arrival, repository, private environment review, AI repair, runtime visibility, remote connection, connected desktop, and closing layouts. Inspected 18 samples around the six major scene changes and extra held frames at 23.5, 28, 32, and 39 seconds.

The initial V2 pass exposed a parent-opacity bug: the outgoing repository title remained visible over the environment title. Multiplying child opacity by its parent's fixed that. The first AI camera crop landed below the repair summary; moving its focus upward and increasing its zoom made the actual repair context readable. The log screenshot initially showed low-value display-server diagnostics. The final asset shows a concise exact excerpt of the recorded rebuild/runtime log instead. The runtime fixture was corrected to the frontend's nested response shape, so the captured status and health result are consistent. Development controls and transient toast notifications were hidden during capture.

Major transitions use one-second optical overlap, with zero-velocity camera endpoints and critically damped type springs. Faster moves use six temporally sampled subframes. Captions stay outside the blur pass. Stable micrograin removes V1's changing-frame sparkle. Deterministic seeking was checked by rendering the same frame before and after a different scene and comparing bytes. The Remotion V2 composition rendered independently with Chrome Headless Shell.

The screenshot plates use intentional close crops; their headlines and key evidence remain the readable story. The real RDP proof is a Linux test desktop, not a fabricated Windows screen. All setup/chat captures use staged demo data in the production frontend. The repair chat is a faithful summary of a recorded real test, not the original transcript.
