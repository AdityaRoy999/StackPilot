# Third-party components

- Motion design/template toolkit: Mort1d/motion-graphics-skills (MIT), https://github.com/Mort1d/motion-graphics-skills. js/engine.js and the scaffold tools/synth were supplied by that skill. Their unused demo scenes are not rendered.
- Motion-design guidance: haidrrrry/claude-remotion-skill (MIT), https://github.com/haidrrrry/claude-remotion-skill.
- Remotion and its official skills: https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion and https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills. Remotion uses its own license; the programmatic Canvas/FFmpeg export does not depend on a Remotion rendering service.
- Local narration: Kokoro ONNX (MIT), https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx. Kokoro-82M model is Apache 2.0. This film uses the af_heart voice, not a cloned person. Model/voice release files remain in the external cache and were verified against the release SHA-256 digests.
- Forced word alignment: stable-ts and OpenAI Whisper, https://github.com/jianfch/stable-ts and https://github.com/openai/whisper (MIT).
- Brand fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono, SIL OFL 1.1. Their license texts are included in public/licenses.
- Music, sound effects, scene code and voiceover script were created for this film. Sarvam's video supplied a style reference via the user's written breakdown; its media and identity are not included.
